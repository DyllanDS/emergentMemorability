CROWDING AMONG THINGS CONCEPTS
================================================================================================
26,107 photographs collapsed to 1,854 concepts (median 13 photographs each).
7,168 concatenated dimensions from 14 network/layer spaces, each z-scored.
Crowding is computed among concept centroids; memorability is averaged
within concept. Spearman rho throughout.

Photographs of one object lie close together in these spaces, so the
nearest neighbors of a photograph are mostly photographs of the same
object. Crowding is therefore measured among concepts, the level at
which the SPoSE embedding describes THINGS.
