REM IN CONTINUOUS RECOGNITION
================================================================================================
g=0.4, w=20, copy=0.7. 4,000 simulated participants, each
inheriting the 295-trial structure of one participant in the original
study: same sequence of first presentations and repeats, same lags, same
proportions of targets, fillers and vigilance repeats. Only the items are
simulated, so they have no memorability of their own.

The criterion is set at each storage probability so that the false alarm
rate is 2%. The model runs at the standard storage probability and at the
storage probability whose overall d' is closest to the participants'
(matched accuracy).
