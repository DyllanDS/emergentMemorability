NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Split-half: the mean Spearman rho between two random halves over the
  splits. Spearman-Brown: 2r / (1 + r) of that mean, the reliability of the
  full sample. A correlation with these scores can be at most about the
  square root of the Spearman-Brown value.
  Corrected recognition is the hit rate minus the false alarm rate.
  All splits draw from one numpy generator with seed 0.
