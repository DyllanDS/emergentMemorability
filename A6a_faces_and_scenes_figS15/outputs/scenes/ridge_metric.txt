NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Each image is scored by its summed similarity to every other image of
  the database. The isotropic metric weights every dimension equally
  (Fig. S15a, equal weights); the ridge-weighted metric weights each
  dimension by its squared ridge coefficient, rescaled to mean 1 (Fig.
  S15a, fitted weights). The highest correlation these scores allow is
  about the square root of the Spearman-Brown value in noise_ceiling.txt.
