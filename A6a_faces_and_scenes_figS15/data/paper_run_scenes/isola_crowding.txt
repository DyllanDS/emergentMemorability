REPRESENTATIONAL CROWDING AND SCENE MEMORABILITY
================================================================================================
10,442 images embedded; 2,222 carry memorability scores.
Ensemble of 14 network/layer spaces, each z-scored before averaging.
Crowding is computed over all images; only CVPR target images enter the correlations.
Spearman rho throughout. Prediction: crowding raises false alarms, lowers hits and d'.

Limiting case: mean ensemble distance to every other image (negated)

  FA rate    rho = +0.129 (p = 1.1e-09)
