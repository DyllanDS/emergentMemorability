REPRESENTATIONAL CROWDING AND FACE MEMORABILITY
================================================================================================
10,168 faces embedded; 2,222 carry memorability scores.
Ensemble of 14 network/layer spaces, each z-scored before averaging.
Crowding is computed over all faces; only scored faces enter the correlations.
Spearman rho throughout. Prediction: crowding raises false alarms, lowers hits and d'.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Limiting case: mean ensemble distance to every other face (negated)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  hit rate   rho = -0.324 (p = 1.81e-55)
  FA rate    rho = +0.301 (p = 6.83e-48)
  d'         rho = -0.458 (p = 2.02e-115)
