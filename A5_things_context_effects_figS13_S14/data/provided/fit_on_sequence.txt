which images are memorable, only what the surrounding stream does.

READING THIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  bin 1 is the least crowded preceding stream, bin 5 the most crowded, and
  bins are formed within image, so the same photograph appears in all five.
  A d-prime drop from bin 1 to bin 5 is caused by the stream, not the item.
  The best single space and falloff is chosen on the fitting half (the largest
  bin 1 minus bin 5 d' drop) and scored on the held-out half in the same way as
  the fitted combination.
