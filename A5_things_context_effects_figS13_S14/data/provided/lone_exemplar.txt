THE LONE-EXEMPLAR CONTRAST, WITHIN IMAGE AND AT MATCHED SERIAL POSITION
====================================================================================================
6,223,953 trials, 21,137 participants; trials require at least 20 preceding items.
A trial is 'lone' when no item of the probe's THINGS higher-level category has yet
appeared in that participant's stream. Rates are standardized to a common
image-by-position distribution (10 position deciles), so the two kinds of
trial are compared over the same images at the same positions. Regressions are
per participant, with the lone indicator centered within image and serial position
(and lag, for hits) entered alongside it.

READING THIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The standardized rows compare lone and not-lone trials over the same images at
  the same serial positions (the image-by-position cells that hold both kinds of
  trial). The regression rows give the same contrast in rate units per
  participant, with inference across participants; the second model adds feature
  crowding (the top-10 SPoSE measure, z-scored and centered within image) to the
  lone indicator. The TYPICAL and ATYPICAL blocks repeat the analysis in the
  halves of the concepts at or above and below the median of a concept's mean
  SPoSE correlation with its ten most similar concepts (over all 1,854
  concepts).
