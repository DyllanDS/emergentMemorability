READING THIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  M1 and M2 enter each measure alone. M3 enters both together, so each
  coefficient is the effect of one measure with the other held constant.
  M4 adds database typicality (the mean of a concept's ten highest SPoSE
  correlations over all 1,854 concepts) and its product with category
  crowding, which asks whether category-mates matter less for concepts that
  are already crowded in the full space.
