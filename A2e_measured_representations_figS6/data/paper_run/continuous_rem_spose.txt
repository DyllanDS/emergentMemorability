READING THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  sigma is the only quantity the continuous case adds, given as a fraction of
  each dimension's standard deviation across the concepts. Fig. S6 shows
  sigma 0.02, 0.03 and 0.05.
  The comparable numbers are: REM in the run of Fig. 1 gives rho =
  -0.551 between memorability and crowding, and the designed-list contrast
  gives d' -0.132.
  rho vs local and rho vs global correlate memorability with crowding over
  the 20 nearest concepts and over all other concepts.
  designed d' and hits-FA are the changes in d' and in hit rate minus false
  alarm rate from 0 to 16 close neighbors on the designed lists.
